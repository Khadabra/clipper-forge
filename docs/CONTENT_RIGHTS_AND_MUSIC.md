# Content Rights, Sources, and Music

This document is product guidance, not legal advice.

## 1. Source video
- Prefer videos the user owns or has permission to process.
- Local upload is the default.
- Online-source integration must not bypass DRM, authentication, paywalls, or platform restrictions.
- A publicly viewable video is not automatically free to download, reuse, or monetize.
- Keep source URL and user-provided rights notes in project metadata where appropriate.
- If a source cannot be accessed through an authorized method, explain that the user can upload a file they are entitled to use.

## 2. Music
- Never scrape or download commercial/trending tracks from unauthorized sources.
- Allow the user to import their own audio files.
- Music library fields: title, creator, source URL/path, license, commercial-use status, attribution text, restrictions, date verified, notes.
- Do not label a track “copyright-free” without verifiable license metadata.
- Separate “mood match” from “trending track”.
- If license is unknown, show a warning and do not represent it as cleared for monetized use.
- AI-generated audio also needs license/terms review before commercial use.
- Provide mute/remove music and speech ducking controls.

## 3. Trend information
- Any trend reference must display its source and checked date.
- If no reliable source is available, call it a style inspiration, not a verified current trend.
- Do not infer platform-wide popularity from one viral example.
- Avoid automating platform scraping in ways that violate terms or privacy.

## 4. Monetization
- Adding subtitles, cropping, or background music alone may not be enough to make reused content eligible for platform monetization.
- Encourage original commentary, meaningful analysis, licensed source material, or permission where appropriate.
- Do not guarantee monetization, reach, or virality.
